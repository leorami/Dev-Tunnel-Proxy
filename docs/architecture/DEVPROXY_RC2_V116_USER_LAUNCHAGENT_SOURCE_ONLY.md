## Apple launchd design choice and tradeoff
Apple's launchd agent rules support per-logged-in-user `~/Library/LaunchAgents`, `ProgramArguments`, `WorkingDirectory`, `StandardOutPath`, `StandardErrorPath`, `RunAtLoad` and `KeepAlive`. Sources:
- https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/MacOSX/Conceptual/BPSystemStartup/Chapters/CreatingLaunchdJobs.html
- https://support.apple.com/guide/terminal/script-management-with-launchd-apdc6c1077b-5d5d-4d35-9c19-60f2397b2369/mac
- https://keith.github.io/xcode-man-pages/launchd.plist.5.html

This design intentionally sets `RunAtLoad:true`, `KeepAlive:false`. That is **login-start once, no automatic restart after crash**. Blind `KeepAlive:true` is unsafe: the worker deliberately preserves singleton/replay evidence on indeterminate exit, and a restart storm cannot override that quarantine. The tradeoff is important: if Docker is unavailable when the user logs in, the worker may fail and will NOT restart automatically later. Do not misrepresent this as complete unattended reboot robustness. A future explicit owner decision can weigh restart/boot readiness against quarantine, but no fallback may auto-clear locks or replay signed operations.
