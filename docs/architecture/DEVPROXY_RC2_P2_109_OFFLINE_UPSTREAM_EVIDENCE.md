## Exact generator source path and new acceptance contract (V117)
Direct CTO code-read of `utils/generateAppsBundle.js` at genuinely returned V116 `10aef4fdc53593ce84e0ac1cd7645048b0326dc4` shows `hardenLocationFullText()` **already converts many static** `proxy_pass http://hostname:port/path` directives to `set $up_... hostname:port;` plus `resolver 127.0.0.11` and `proxy_pass http://$up_.../path`.
